Hey, here's how our week usually goes.

We're open for lunch and dinner every day. Weekday lunch is 11 to 2, pretty chill, one cook on the line and one person on register is fine. Weekday dinners run 5 to 9 and we need a cook, a cashier, and someone running expo.

Fridays are different. Dinner goes til 10 and it's slammed, so 2 cooks plus a cashier and expo. Friday lunch is the same as the other weekdays.

Weekends: lunch is 11-3 and needs 2 on the grill, 1 cashier, 1 expo. Weekend dinner is 5-10 with 2 on the grill, 2 on register, 1 expo.

Base pay: line cooks $17/hr, cashiers $14, expo $15.
